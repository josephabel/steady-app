Steady

Turn your glucose data into clear, everyday insight.

Steady reads glucose, insulin, carb, and workout data from Apple Health and does the analysis for you — automatic meal breakdowns, time in range, highs and lows, workout impact, long-term trends, and doctor-ready reports. Everything stays on your device.

Common questions

  • Steady shows no data / thin charts. Steady reads from Apple Health, so your CGM app needs to be sharing into Apple Health first. Check Apple Health → Blood Glucose → Data Sources & Access. Dexcom, FreeStyle Libre, Eversense, and Loop/iAPS/Trio can all share into Apple Health.
  • No CGM data yet? You can explore the whole app with built-in sample data — tap “Explore with sample data” on first launch, or go to Settings → Sample Data. A “SAMPLE DATA” banner shows whenever it’s on so it’s never mistaken for your real readings.
  • Is my data private? Yes — your health data stays on your device. See the privacy policy below.
  • Which devices are supported? iPhone (with an optional Apple Watch app and Home Screen widgets). Steady is iPhone-only and requires your CGM to be sharing into Apple Health.
